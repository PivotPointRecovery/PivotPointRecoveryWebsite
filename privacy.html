Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2, 2026

The Short Version

This website collects information only when you choose to give it to us — by sending a contact message, expressing interest in volunteering, or making a donation. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, ever. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

This Policy Covers the Public Website Only

This policy applies to pivotpointrecovery.org, our public website. It does not cover our staff portal or the confidential participant records kept there, which are governed by separate policies and by federal law — including HIPAA and 42 CFR Part 2, the rule protecting substance use disorder treatment records.

Please do not send confidential health information through this website. The contact and volunteer forms are ordinary web forms and are not a secure channel for clinical or treatment details. If you need to share something sensitive, contact us and we will find an appropriate way to receive it.

What We Collect

Contact form. Your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, the topic you select, and your message.

Volunteer form. Your name, email address, phone number, city, the volunteer areas you're interested in, your general availability, and anything you write in the experience field.

Donations. Your name, email address, the amount, the fund you designate, and whether you indicated an employer match. We never see or store your card number. Payment details are entered on Stripe's own checkout page and handled entirely by Stripe. What comes back to us is a confirmation that a payment succeeded and a reference number.

Automatically. Our host records standard web server logs, and we store the browser user-agent string alongside form submissions to help us identify automated spam.

Why We Collect It

To answer your message, to follow up about volunteering, to process and acknowledge your donation, to meet our recordkeeping and reporting obligations as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and to keep the site working and free of abuse. That's the whole list.

Who We Share It With

We share information only with the service providers that make the site function, and only to the extent they need it:

Stripe — payment processing. Supabase — the database and application layer where submissions are stored. Resend — delivers the notification email that alerts our staff to a new submission. Cloudflare — hosts and serves this website. Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on these pages.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to, or if we believe in good faith that doing so is necessary to protect someone's safety. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.

How Long We Keep It

Contact messages and volunteer interest submissions are kept as long as they remain useful to our work, and are reviewed periodically. Donation records are kept for at least seven years, because nonprofit financial and tax recordkeeping requires it.

How It's Protected

The site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions travel encrypted and are written to a database that is not publicly readable — the tables holding your information have row-level security enabled and no public access policies, so they can only be reached by our own server-side code. Access is limited to staff who need it.

No system is perfectly secure, and we won't claim otherwise. We do take it seriously.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Our host, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary cookie for security and bot protection. If you go through donation checkout, Stripe sets cookies on its own pages as part of processing your payment and preventing fraud.

Your Choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — subject to records we're legally required to retain, such as donation history. Email info@pivotpointrecovery.org or use our contact form and we'll take care of it.

Recurring donors can cancel a monthly gift at any time by contacting us. We will not make you jump through hoops.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information through it. If you believe a child has submitted information here, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of the page. Material changes will be noted clearly rather than slipped in quietly.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or about information we hold? Email info@pivotpointrecovery.org or reach us through the contact page.
