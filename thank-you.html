Thank You

Your Gift Is On Its Way to Work

You just made peer recovery support possible for someone in Northern Virginia.

What Happens Next

Stripe is emailing your receipt now — keep it for your tax records. Pivot Point Recovery, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-4331928, and your donation is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

If your employer matches charitable gifts, submit their matching form whenever you're ready and we'll provide any documentation they ask for. Get in touch and we'll handle it.

Where Your Money Actually Goes

Every Pivot Point service is free to the person receiving it. There is no sliding scale and no insurance requirement, because the people who need us most are usually the ones who can least afford another bill. Gifts like yours are the entire reason that's possible.

Your support pays for transportation to a court date, an hour with a Certified Peer Recovery Specialist, a reentry starter kit for someone walking out of incarceration with nothing — the concrete, unglamorous things that decide whether a recovery holds.
